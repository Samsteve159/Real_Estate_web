Manifest Buyer App03 · What it looks like
The spine of the app

Seven stages, start to keys

Each stage carries its own tools, interactive checklists and reminders. Nothing is forgotten, because the app remembers for them.

1

Get Ready

"Can I afford it — and what's my budget?"
Borrowing capacity · Deposit · Stamp duty · Budget · Savings tracker · Grants check · connect to a broker · Buyer Readiness Score
2

Find a Property

"Is this the right one — and is it fairly priced?"
Inspection checklist · Questions to ask the agent · Comparable sales · Suburb score · School zones · Flood/fire & development overlays · Rental yield · Resale estimate
3

Before You Offer

"How do I not overpay or get burned?"
Offer & auction strategy · Recommended offer range · Due-diligence checklist · Building & pest booking · Conveyancer · Finance-clause explained
4

Under Contract

"Am I on track to settlement?"
Progress tracker: contract signed → deposit paid → finance approved → inspections → Section 32 reviewed → settlement booked → utilities → insurance
5

Final Inspection

"Is the home as promised?"
Interactive checklist with photo capture — appliances, heating/cooling, hot water, fixtures included, damage since inspection, keys
6·7

Settlement & After

"Keys today — and settled in for the long run."
Settlement tracker · key collection · utility transfers · then address-change reminders, maintenance schedule & a property-value tracker

Manifest Buyer App05 · Why it wins
Free for buyers — how it pays for itself

The money is on the other side

A first-home buyer with no spare cash gets the full journey, tools and everyday mentor for free. We earn from the supply side and from optional power features — never from the anxious buyer.

1
Partner network Live v1 · curated

Vetted building & pest, conveyancers, brokers, insurance, utilities. Buyers get discounts; we take a referral margin. The two-sided flywheel and the reason they stay.

2
Paid AI boosts Buyer-side

Optional 1-day / 1-week / 1-month passes for heavy AI at the moment of stress — contract-clause review, building-report summaries, negotiation coach, auction-week mentor.

3
Agent & broker leads to Manifest Direct win

Every "talk to a human" tap is a qualified, high-intent lead. The app is a lead engine for Manifest's core business — it pays back before partner revenue does.

4
Business portal + white-label v2

Self-serve partner sign-up with referral tracking, then licensing the whole engine to other agencies as their branded buyer app. Manifest becomes a platform.

Two portals. The buyer app (v1) + a business portal where partners publish offers and referrals are tracked (v2). Everything runs under a ≤ $500/month cost cap — premium property data is deferred to a paid tier so free stays sustainable.
Manifest Buyer App06 · How it stays free
Building on the vision

Ideas that make it sticky

Extensions of Akshay's core idea — the ones marked ★ are the high-impact bets for the early roadmap.

★Buyer Readiness Score

One confidence meter that fills as Stage 1 completes — turns "am I ready?" anxiety into a game with a clear next step.

★Document Vault

Store the contract, Section 32, building & pest reports in-app — and let the mentor read them: "any red flags here?"

★Ask-before-you-sign

AI contract-clause explainer with red-flag detection — short cooling-off, finance-clause traps, unusual special conditions.

◆Auction-day companion

Walk-away price locked in beforehand, live bid-increment calculator, and calm-nerve prompts so no one over-bids.

★Property School

Bite-size lessons ("what's a Section 32?"). Drives daily engagement, app-store keywords and SEO growth.

★Invite-a-friend

Both sides unlock a partner discount. Buyers are surrounded by other buyers — the cheapest growth channel there is.

◆Shortlist compare

Save several properties and compare price, yield, overlays and suburb score side-by-side.

★Post-settlement stickiness

Maintenance schedule, value tracker, and a refinance nudge years later — re-engagement and a fresh broker lead.
