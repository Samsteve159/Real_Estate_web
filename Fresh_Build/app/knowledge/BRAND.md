# BRAND — name, voice & design system

## Positioning
A **standalone consumer brand, powered by Manifest Real Estate.** The app brand is
warm, empowering and trustworthy — a mentor, not a salesperson. Manifest appears as
the credible backer ("powered by Manifest Real Estate") and the human backstop.

- **Homeward** — the journey-to-a-home feeling.

## Tagline
Primary working line: **"Your personal buyer, in your pocket."**
Alternates: *"Buy your first home with confidence." · "Every step to the keys." ·
"The buyer's advocate that fits in your phone."*

## Voice & tone
- **Mentor, not marketer.** Calm, plain-English, reassuring. Explains the jargon.
- **On the buyer's side, always.** We work for *them*, never the seller.
- **Confident but honest** — "indicative", "here's how we worked it out", "talk to a
  human for this one."
- Short sentences. No hype. No dark patterns.

## Visual system (inherited from Manifest V2 — LOCKED)
Dark, premium, gold-jeweled. Typeface **Raleway**.

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| bg | `#0a0a0b` | near-black page background |
| surface | `#141416` | cards / elevated |
| surface-2 | `#1c1c20` | raised / hovered |
| text | `#f5f5f6` | primary white |
| muted | `#a1a1aa` | body / secondary |
| dim | `#71717a` | tertiary / disabled |
| **gold** | **`#c2a267`** | the jewel — one CTA/view, key numbers, eyebrows |
| gold-bright | `#d4b87a` | gradient highlight |
| navy | `#003970` | deep secondary tone, sparingly |
| line | `rgba(255,255,255,0.10)` | hairlines |

**Usage rule:** ~80% black/white, ~10–15% surfaces, **≤5% gold**. Gold is the jewel —
overuse kills it. Wide-tracked uppercase gold **eyebrow** (0.28em) on sections is the
"premium tell". Display headlines Raleway 600, tight tracking `-0.01em`; body off-
white, line-height ~1.65; ≤3 weights.

**Mobile adaptation:** larger tap targets, bottom tab bar, thumb-reachable primary
actions, generous spacing. Keep the dark+gold restraint — resist the urge to add
color per stage; use gold + a single subtle accent for progress.
