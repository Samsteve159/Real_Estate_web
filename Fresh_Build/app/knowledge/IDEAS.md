# IDEAS — building on Akshay's vision (living)

Akshay's core vision is the spine: empower buyers end-to-end + partner discounts as the
hook. These ideas extend it. Marked ★ = high-impact / recommended for early roadmap.

## Deepen the "mentor in your pocket"
- ★ **Buyer Readiness Score** — one confidence meter that fills as Stage-1 items
  complete. Turns "am I ready?" anxiety into a game with a clear next step.
- ★ **Document Vault** — store the contract, Section 32, building/pest reports and
  receipts in-app. Doubles as the AI mentor's context ("summarise this report",
  "any red flags in this Section 32?").
- ★ **Ask-before-you-sign** — AI contract-clause explainer with **red-flag detection**
  (unusual special conditions, short cooling-off, finance-clause traps).
- **Auction-day companion** — walk-away price locked in beforehand, live bid-increment
  calculator, and calm-nerve prompts ("your limit is $X, don't chase").
- **Shortlist compare** — save multiple properties, compare price/yield/overlays/score
  side by side; the app remembers what you liked.
- **Inspection mode** — on-site checklist with photo capture, voice notes, and GPS-tag
  so each property's evidence is filed automatically.

## Trust & credibility (the make-or-break)
- ★ **"How we worked this out"** on every number — expandable formula + official
  source. This is the anti-ChatGPT moat.
- **Verified partner badges + real reviews** — never pay-to-rank; buyers see why a
  partner is trusted.
- **One-tap human backstop** — "talk to a real expert" (Manifest) on any high-stakes
  step. Trust + lead-gen in one.
- **Grants & concessions finder** — auto-checks FHOG + VIC stamp-duty concession
  eligibility from the buyer's Stage-1 inputs (real money found = instant trust).

## Growth & retention loops
- ★ **Invite-a-friend** — both sides get a partner discount. Buyers are surrounded by
  other buyers.
- **QR at open homes / partner shopfronts** — "scan to check if this place is fairly
  priced." Distribution at the point of need.
- ★ **Property School** — bite-size lessons ("what's a Section 32?", "how auctions
  really work"). Drives SEO, ASO keywords, and daily engagement.
- **Price/suburb watch + alerts** — keeps the app on the home screen between properties.
- ★ **Post-settlement stickiness** — maintenance schedule, warranty/receipt vault,
  **equity/value tracker**, and a later **refinance nudge** (re-engagement + a fresh
  broker lead years after purchase).

## Monetization extensions (beyond the core)
- **Tiered partner categories** — high-margin ones (broking, insurance, conveyancing,
  depreciation, solar) prioritised in the directory.
- **Sponsored-but-labelled** partner placements (clearly disclosed).
- **"Pro" freemium** — power features (unlimited AI, deep suburb reports) for a small
  monthly fee; core stays free.
- **White-label licensing** — license the whole engine to *other* agencies as their
  branded buyer app (Manifest becomes a platform, not just an agency). Big future line.
- **Anonymised journey insights** — aggregated, privacy-safe demand data is valuable to
  developers/agents (only if it never compromises buyer trust).

## Product-strategy bets
- **Start narrow, own it completely** — FHB + VIC + the full journey beats "all buyers,
  all states, shallow". Depth is the differentiator ChatGPT can't copy.
- **The app is a wedge for Manifest's core business** — every engaged buyer is a future
  seller, a broker lead, and a referral source. The app pays for itself in leads even
  before partner revenue.
- **Data + relationships compound** — the longer a buyer uses it, the better it knows
  them, and the more valuable the human handoff becomes.

## Parked / later
- Investor mode (cash-flow, capital growth, depreciation) once FHB is proven.
- Other states as templated stamp-duty/overlay modules.
- Agent-side companion (the seller's journey) — a whole second product.
