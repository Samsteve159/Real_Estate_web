# COMMERCIALS — what we charge the client (service fees)

> **This is separate from `MONETIZATION.md`.** MONETIZATION = how the *app* earns
> from buyers/partners (buyer core stays free). **COMMERCIALS = the service fee we
> charge the client (Manifest / Akshay) to build & run the product for them.**
> Final figures are **set by Akshay** — the numbers below are the reference model,
> not locked pricing.

## The charging model (reference — from the website)
The delivered website is priced on a simple base + management, or a once-off:

**Normal website (1–2 live pages)**
| Option | Structure | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **1 · Subscription** | $155/mo base **+** $6/mo management (incl. **1 change / month**) | **$161 / mo** → **$1,932 / yr** |
| **2 · Once-off** | Pay once, build & hand over | **$1,800** |
