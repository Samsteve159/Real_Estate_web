# MONETIZATION — free to buyers, money on the other side

> Not to be confused with **`COMMERCIALS.md`** — that's the *service fee we charge
> the client (Akshay) to build & run the product*. This doc is how the **app itself**
> earns from buyers/partners.

**Principle: the buyer core is free forever.** A first-home buyer with no money to
spare must get the full journey + tools + everyday AI mentor at no cost. We make money
from the *supply side* of the market and from optional power features.

## Revenue streams

### 1. Partner network — the killer hook (primary)
A **verified** directory of the services every buyer needs, with **member discounts**.
We earn a **referral margin / commission** per booking.
- Building & pest inspectors · conveyancers / solicitors · mortgage brokers ·
  home & contents insurance · utility connection · removalists · depreciation
  schedules · solar/energy · locksmiths · cleaners.
- Buyer wins (real savings + vetted providers). Partner wins (qualified, high-intent
  customers at the exact moment of need). We win (margin). **Two-sided flywheel.**
- **v1 = curated directory (hand-picked, manual referral).** **v2 = self-serve
  business portal** where partners onboard, publish offers, and referrals/commissions
  are tracked (this is the second portal).

### 2. Paid AI boosts (optional, buyer-side)
Time-boxed passes for heavy AI when it matters most:
- **Contract-clause review pass**, **building-report summary**, **negotiation coach**,
  **auction-week unlimited mentor**.
- **1 day / 1 week / 1 month** pricing. Impulse-priced around the moment of stress.

### 3. Agent / broker lead-gen to Manifest (and a broker panel)
Every "talk to a human" / "connect to a broker" tap is a **qualified lead**. Routes to
Manifest (and later a panel of vetted agents/brokers). This is Manifest's direct win
from backing the app.

### 4. Premium data tier (later)
Deep comparable-sales / suburb reports beyond the free basics — if/when we license paid
data. Gated so free-tier stays useful.

### 5. Later / optional
Clearly-labelled sponsored partner placements · a "Pro" freemium tier for power
features · **white-label licensing** of the engine to other agencies · aggregated,
anonymised journey insights.

## Two portals (confirmed)
1. **Buyer app** (v1) — everything above, buyer-facing.
2. **Business / partner portal** (v2) — partners sign up, publish member offers, and
   receive/track referrals + commissions. Needs accounts, a DB, and referral tracking.

## Cost discipline
Everything stays within the **≤ $500/mo** cap. Anthropic usage is gated by the Manifest
workspace spend cap. The only line that could threaten the cap is **premium property
data** (Domain/CoreLogic) — deferred to a paid tier; v1 uses free gov data + curated
comps + the existing AI valuation. See `ROADMAP.md` for the running-cost breakdown.

## Why buyers won't resent the money model
They never pay for the core. The paid pieces are optional and genuinely valuable at the
moment of need, and the partner discounts *save them more than we make.* The incentives
line up: we only win when the buyer wins.
