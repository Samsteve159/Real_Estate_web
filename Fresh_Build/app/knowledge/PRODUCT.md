# PRODUCT — vision, niche & positioning

## The one-liner
**A personal buyer in your pocket.** The one app that walks a buyer through the
*entire* property purchase — from "am I ready?" to "I've moved in" — in one place
they can actually trust.

## The gap (why this exists)
There is **no single app that holds a buyer's hand from start to finish.** Buyers —
especially first-timers — don't know who to trust, and have to stitch information
together from a dozen fragmented sources (portals, calculators, forums, agents who
work for the seller, random blogs). Every existing app does one narrow slice.
Nobody owns the whole journey. **This app does.**

## Who it's for (v1)
**First-home buyers in Victoria.** They are the most anxious, least-informed, and
most in need of a trusted guide. The full 7-stage hand-holding + mentor tone is
built for them. Expand to investors and upgraders (and other states) later.

## Positioning
A **standalone consumer brand, *powered by Manifest Real Estate.*** Manifest is the
credibility and the human backstop (talk to a real expert); the app is agency-
neutral so it can grow nationally without reading as "one agency's app."

## The 8 strategic questions — our answers

**1. Why use this over ChatGPT / a generic AI tool?**
Because it's a **system of action, not a chat box.** ChatGPT gives you words; this
gives you:
- A **structured end-to-end journey** with a timeline, reminders and interactive
  checklists that actually track *your* purchase.
- **Deterministic calculators** that are provably correct and **cite official
  sources** (SRO, gov data) — not a hallucinated guess.
- **Real property + overlay data** (comparable sales, flood/fire/development
  overlays, school zones) tied to the actual address you're looking at.
- A **human backstop** — one tap to a real Manifest expert for anything high-stakes.
- **Member-only partner discounts** (building & pest, conveyancing, broking,
  insurance) that no AI chatbot can give you.
ChatGPT can't remember your settlement date, remind you to book a pest inspection,
guarantee a stamp-duty figure, or get you $100 off a building inspection. This can.

**2. How trustworthy is the information?**
- **Calculators are deterministic** and show their working + the official source.
- **AI answers are grounded, cite provenance, and carry "indicative" disclaimers**;
  anything legally/financially high-stakes routes to a human.
- Property data is labelled with its source and freshness.
- We never present an estimate as a valuation, or AI output as legal/financial advice.

**3. How do we make consumers trust it?**
Visible source citations on every number · Manifest's real track record + real agent
reviews · privacy-first (local-first storage, minimal PII, no selling data) · a
*verified* partner network (not pay-to-rank) · zero dark patterns · a real human one
tap away.

**4. The pitch / niche.**
FHB-first. *"Your personal buyer's advocate, in your pocket — free."* We empower the
anxious first-timer to act with the confidence of someone who's bought ten homes.

**5. How is it free yet amazing?**
The **buyer core is free forever.** We monetize the *other* side of the market:
partner referral commissions + member discounts (two-sided), **paid AI boosts**
(contract review, negotiation, auction week), and agent/broker lead-gen to Manifest.
The buyer never pays for the core journey. (See `MONETIZATION.md`.)

**6. How do we grow reach?**
App-store ASO · FHB communities + first-home-buyer seminars · mortgage-broker &
buyer's-agent partnerships as distribution · referral incentives (invite a friend →
both get perks) · content/SEO ("Property School") · Manifest's own channels · QR
codes at open homes and partner shopfronts.

**7. Timeline, reminders, interactive checklists.**
These are the **core engine**, not add-ons — a local-first journey engine with
push reminders tied to the buyer's real dates. (See `JOURNEY.md`.)

**8. The killer hook: real 3rd-party discounts for members.**
Verified partner network with **member discounts** on building & pest, conveyancers,
brokers, insurance, utility connection, removalists, depreciation schedules, etc.
Buyers get real savings; partners get qualified customers; we take a referral margin.
**v1 = curated directory (manual); v2 = self-serve business portal + referral
tracking + paid boosts.** This is why a buyer keeps (and recommends) the app.

## What "winning" looks like
A first-home buyer downloads it the day they start thinking about buying, and it's
the app they open at every step until the day they get the keys — and beyond
(maintenance, value tracking, refinance). They tell three friends because it saved
them money and stress.
